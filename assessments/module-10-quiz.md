# Module 10 quiz

Nine scenario questions on preference tuning and measuring it: choosing preference data, the
reference model and beta, reading DPO metrics without fooling yourself, judge cost and choice,
position and length bias, confidence intervals, and the regression check against base skills.
Pass mark: 70% (7 of 9).

Before you start, you should have finished:

- [10.1 · Preference data and the DPO run](../lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md)
- [10.2 · Measuring alignment with a judge](../lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md)

The practical result of this module is your aligned model under `hf/`, `reports/winrate.json` and
`reports/alignment_results.md`. The quiz checks the decisions behind them.
