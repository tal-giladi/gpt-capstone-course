# Module 6 quiz

Nine scenario questions on turning a GPT-2 reproduction into your own recipe with evidence: what
makes an ablation fair, how much of a difference is noise, reading architecture and optimizer
results (including what the public evidence on Muon and WSD does and does not show), the export
rule, and sizing and pricing the main run before you freeze it. Pass mark: 70% (7 of 9).

Before you start, you should have finished:

- [06.1 · Designing a fair ablation](../lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- [06.2 · Architecture ablations](../lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)
- [06.3 · Optimizer and schedule ablations](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)
- [06.4 · Sizing the main run and freezing the recipe](../lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md)

The practical result of this module is `results/ablations.md`, the frozen
`configs/main_350m.yaml` at tag `recipe-v1` and its decision record. The quiz checks the
reasoning behind them.
