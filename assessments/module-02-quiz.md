# Module 2 quiz

Nine scenario questions on getting pretraining data the right way: choosing and licensing the
corpus, sizing it, picking and securing object storage, and moving ~93 GB into your bucket on a
rented box without losing data or money. Pass mark: 70% (7 of 9).

Before you start, you should have finished:

- [02.1 · Choosing pretraining data](../lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- [02.2 · Object storage for datasets and checkpoints](../lessons/module-02/lesson-02.md)
- [02.3 · Downloading FineWeb-Edu into your bucket](../lessons/module-02/lesson-03.md)

The practical result of this module is in your bucket and your repo: verified raw shards,
manifests and `DATA_CARD.md`. The quiz checks the decisions behind them.
