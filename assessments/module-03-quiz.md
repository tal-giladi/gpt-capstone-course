# Module 3 quiz

Nine scenario questions on turning raw text into training-ready tokens: choosing a vocabulary size
and training sample, special tokens, sizing and running tokenization on a rented CPU box, checking
shards, keeping validation data held out, and finding and removing benchmark contamination.
Pass mark: 70% (7 of 9).

Before you start, you should have finished:

- [03.1 · Training your own BPE tokenizer](../lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- [03.2 · Tokenizing 20B tokens on a rented CPU box](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- [03.3 · Validation split, decontamination and the token report](../lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)

The practical result of this module is in your bucket and your repo: the tokenizer, four checked
shard sets and `TOKEN_REPORT.md`. The quiz checks the decisions behind them.
