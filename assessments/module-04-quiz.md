# Module 4 quiz

Nine scenario questions on renting GPUs without wasting money: comparing providers and hardware,
billing traps, checking a fresh box before trusting it, automatic stops, and turning benchmark
numbers into a budget you can defend. Pass mark: 70% (7 of 9).

Before you start, you should have finished:

- [04.1 · Where to rent GPUs and what they cost](../lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)
- [04.2 · Your first H100 box](../lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- [04.3 · Benchmarking throughput and fixing the budget](../lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md)

The practical result of this module is your reusable `mygpt-gpu` template, an environment record,
a benchmark table and a revised `budget.md`. The quiz checks the decisions behind them.
